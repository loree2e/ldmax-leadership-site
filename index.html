Why "LD Max"

Every team has a point of maximum performance.

Pilots learn it early: at one precise speed, the aircraft gets its best glide, its longest range, its most performance for the least effort. Fly slower and you're plowing. Fly faster and you're burning energy fighting the air. That point is called L/D max.

Leadership teams work the same way. Most are carrying drag they've stopped noticing: unclear priorities, unspoken conflict, meetings that produce heat instead of lift. The work of LD Max Leadership is to find that drag, name it, and trim it, so the energy your team already has starts converting into altitude.

LD Max Leadership was built at that intersection. Our founder flew jets off carriers and led teams through hypergrowth in Silicon Valley, and our coaches have guided executives through the moments when the stakes are highest.

The Framework

SOAR: how good leaders become great ones.

From founder Loree Draude's book SOAR into Joy, tested with leadership teams from the flight deck to Fortune 500 boardrooms. It's one thing to fly, to just drone along at one altitude. We want to SOAR.

S

Self-Awareness

Know your values and your strengths. A strength is something you do well and love doing. Most leaders are guessing at both.

O

Openness

Be open to feedback and to being wrong. Pilots who rejected their landing grades got sent home. The ones who listened made the top of the board.

A

Appreciation

The number one reason people leave jobs is not feeling appreciated. Appreciation is a leadership practice, and it's trainable.

R

Responsibility

Aviate, navigate, communicate. Taking 100 percent responsibility is where a leader's freedom actually comes from.

About the Founder

Loree "Rowdy" Draude

Loree Draude was one of the first women in the U.S. Navy to fly in a combat squadron, deploying twice aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln with 300+ carrier landings and a place in the top 10 percent of her air wing as its first woman-qualified landing signal officer. She then spent 20 years leading teams in Silicon Valley, including at Google and Meta, where her leadership practices raised her organization's recognition scores 16 percent.

She holds an MBA from Wharton, is a Harvard Advanced Leadership Initiative fellow, an ICF-certified professional coach (PCC), and the author of two books, including the Amazon bestseller SOAR into Joy. Her one-woman show about her Navy years, I Feel the Need, has played Hollywood Fringe and off-Broadway.

Loree has also been laid off, forced to shut down her startup, divorced, fired, and quit her job. She keeps a written list of her failures and shares it with the leaders she coaches. She also overuses commas.
