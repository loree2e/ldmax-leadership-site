## LinkedIn post

I'm starting a new chapter, and I wanted you all to be among the first to know.

For the past few years, I've had the honor of coaching executives one-on-one, and I kept noticing the same thing: a leader can grow enormously in our sessions, and then walk back into a team that's still carrying the same old drag. The leaders who soar highest are the ones whose whole team is climbing with them.

So I'm shifting my focus. My new company is called LD Max Leadership, and it partners with organizations to develop their leadership teams, through team development programs, executive coaching, and keynotes.

The name comes from aviation. L/D max is the speed where a wing produces the most lift for the least drag. It's the point where everything is working in your favor. Every pilot learns to find it, and I believe every leadership team can find theirs.

And true to my own advice that no bird (or person) soars on its own, I'm not doing this alone. I'm building a bench of executive coaches I know and trust, and partnering with the wonderful team at Day Six Leadership on larger leadership development programs.

I'll still be giving keynotes, and I'll still be in the cockpit telling stories about the night the lights went out at 60 feet. Some things don't change.

Thank you to every client who trusted me with their growth over the past few years. You taught me as much as I taught you, and this new chapter exists because of what I learned alongside you.

If your organization is thinking about how to develop its leaders, I'd love to talk: ldmaxleadership.com
